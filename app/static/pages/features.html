Features

Everything MarkMute can remove — with proof

MarkMute is built on the open-source watermarks-remover toolkit and wraps each detector with clear, confidence-rated reporting. Nothing is silently removed: every run tells you what was found, what was cleaned and what may remain.

Text: invisible Unicode & homoglyph removal

Paste any text and MarkMute scans every character for a broad set of hidden-signal codepoints:

  • Layer A (always): zero-width space/joiner/non-joiner, bidi controls (LRE/RLE/LRO/RLI…), tag characters, variation selectors, word-joiner, BOM/ZWNBSP, soft hyphen, Arabic letter mark — plus space homoglyphs (tabs, ideographic spaces).
  • Layer B (aggressive mode): fullwidth lookalikes and Latin/Cyrillic confusables that survive visual inspection.
  • Paranoid option: strips load-bearing vs16/zwj/tag sequences that would otherwise break emoji rendering.
  • Rewrite: optional LLM paraphrase (via your own Ollama or OpenAI-compatible backend, or a printed prompt) aimed at statistical watermark resistance — best-effort by definition.

Every hit is reported with its codepoint, kind and confidence, and the cleaned result re-counts invisibles so you can verify the outcome.

Images: metadata, C2PA, SynthID & pixel watermarks

  • Metadata cleaning: EXIF/XMP/ICC removal, thumbnail stripping, GPS and history cleanup — pixels are never altered.
  • C2PA provenance: removes manifest chunks from supported formats and re-runs verification; any residual signature is flagged explicitly.
  • SynthID scoring (optional hardware): scores how strongly an image carries SynthID signals when the reverse-SynthID checkout is installed.
  • CtrlRegen pixel removal (optional GPU): inpainting-based regeneration of pixel-watermarked regions via the noai-watermark engine, run as an async job with live progress.

Files & documents: metadata and AI signatures

  • PNG, JPEG, WebP, SVG, PDF, DOCX, ODT, HTML, MD, TXT — up to 256 MiB.
  • EXIF/XMP stripping with encrypted-embedding removal where detectable.
  • C2PA and mutagen tooling for documents that carry provenance chains.
  • Best-effort residual reporting: after cleaning, the same detectors re-run so you see exactly what remains.

Privacy-preserving by design

  • No account, no sign-up, no tracking cookies.
  • Core cleaning never sends your content anywhere; it only touches your server.
  • LLM rewrite is opt-in and asks for confirmation before sending anything.
  • Full details on the privacy page.
