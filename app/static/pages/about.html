About MarkMute

MarkMute started from a simple observation: AI-generated and shared content today carries an invisible biography — hidden characters, provenance manifests, camera and tool metadata. Removing it should be free, private and honest about what it can and cannot do.

Why we built it

Writers find invisible Unicode in ghost-written replies. Creators find SynthID or C2PA provenance in their own exported assets. Buyers find GPS coordinates in photos they paid for. In every case, existing tools either cost money, upload your content to someone else's server, or promise 100% removal they cannot prove.

MarkMute wraps the battle-tested open-source watermarks-remover toolkit in a clean interface that does the opposite of overpromising: every run shows its work — what was detected, what was cleaned, and what residual signals remain.

Our principles

  • Privacy first. No account, no tracking, and core cleaning never sends your content off your own infrastructure.
  • Best-effort with proof. We never claim 100% watermark-free output. Residue gets flagged, not hidden.
  • Built on open source. We stand on the shoulders of the watermarks-remover community work.
  • Responsible use. Remove watermarks from content you own or have rights to — local laws apply.
